Why your wait can change

People are seen by urgency, not arrival time. Ambulance arrivals can push waits up; free rooms and extra staff bring them down.

In a treatment room? Ask reception about text updates on your tests and wait.

Feeling worse while you wait? Tell the triage nurse straight away. Don't leave without telling a staff member.
