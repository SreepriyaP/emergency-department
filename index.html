ED wait-time communication: proof of concept

A waiting-room display board and patient text updates for an emergency department. Everything here uses synthetic data from a simulated hospital (Riverside General is fictional). It runs entirely in your browser, with no internet needed.

Driven by the prediction models

Waiting room board: replay

Replay Sat 28 Nov minute by minute. Every 5 minutes a LightGBM model, trained on 22 months of history, picks each person's most likely 30-minute wait window. Turn on "Show what really happened" to compare with real waits.

 Text updates: replay

Follow eight patients through their visit. Every test is re-estimated every 10 minutes; patients get an update text if their window moves. Each prediction is checked against what happened.

Concept demos

Waiting room board: what-if demo

Press buttons for an ambulance arrival, a free room or an extra clinician and watch the estimates change (simple rule, not the trained model).

 Text update generator

Pick a patient and an event to see the text that would be sent, with SMS length and safety checks.

On a phone? The text update screens work best on a phone. The boards are designed for a waiting-room TV: turn your phone sideways, or open them on a laptop.

Tips: the boards are sized for a 1920×1080 TV and scale to fit your window; press F11 for full screen. On the board replay, Space plays or pauses and the arrow keys step 5 minutes. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online; offline they fall back to your system font.
